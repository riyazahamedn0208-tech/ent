Dr. Kannappan Alagappan, M.S. (ENT)

Medical Director & Consultant ENT Surgeon

Dr. Kannappan Alagappan is a Consultant ENT Surgeon and Medical Director, with more than a decade of experience in Otorhinolaryngology. A dynamic and committed clinician, he combines contemporary ENT knowledge with meticulous surgical practice and a deeply patient-centred approach to care. His constant pursuit of learning enables him to bring modern, evidence-based treatment to his patients.

He believes surgical excellence is built on three pillars — sound clinical judgement, meticulous technique and appropriate use of technology — adopting newer techniques only when they genuinely improve diagnosis, safety, recovery or outcomes. Teaching is one of his deepest professional interests, reflecting his commitment to the next generation of doctors and surgeons.

"World-class care should not be a privilege. It should be accessible to everyone."
