Beyond Routine ENT Care

Exclusive & Advanced ENT Services at AK ENT Zone

At AK ENT Zone, the ENT unit of Palniandavar Nursing Home, we provide a range of advanced and specialised ENT services with a focus on minimally invasive techniques, precise diagnosis and patient-centred treatment.

Endoscopic Otology Neuro-Otology Deglutology
01 — Ear Surgery

Endoscopic Otology

We specialise in endoscopic ear surgery, using high-definition endoscopic technology for the evaluation and management of a wide range of ear pathologies. Wherever clinically appropriate, we favour minimally invasive endoscopic approaches to provide precise visualisation while reducing surgical trauma.

02 — Vertigo & Balance

Advanced Neuro-Otology & Vertigo Care

Our neuro-otology services include comprehensive evaluation of vertigo and balance disorders, incorporating a range of diagnostic and therapeutic positional manoeuvres. Appropriate repositioning and vestibular manoeuvres are tailored to the underlying disorder to provide effective, evidence-based management.

03 — Swallowing Disorders

Deglutology — Advanced Swallowing & Dysphagia Care

AK ENT Zone offers specialised assessment of swallowing disorders (dysphagia). We undertake systematic swallowing evaluation to identify the underlying cause and develop individualised treatment strategies, with the aim of improving swallowing safety, efficiency and quality of life.

04 — OPD Procedure

OPD-Based Intratympanic Therapy for Sudden Sensorineural Hearing Loss

For appropriately selected patients with sudden sensorineural hearing loss, we offer intratympanic medication administration in the outpatient setting. This allows treatment to be performed conveniently under controlled clinical conditions without the need for routine hospital admission.

05 — OPD Excision

Endoscopic OPD Excision of External Ear Lesions

Selected lesions of the external ear can be managed using endoscopic excision under appropriate outpatient conditions. At AK ENT Zone, we offer complete excision of suitable lesions with endoscopic visualisation, allowing precise identification and removal while avoiding unnecessary extensive surgery whenever clinically appropriate.

Our Approach

Precision ENT Care with Modern Technology

Our approach is centred on combining advanced endoscopic techniques, specialised ENT expertise and evidence-based treatment to deliver precise and minimally invasive care whenever appropriate.
